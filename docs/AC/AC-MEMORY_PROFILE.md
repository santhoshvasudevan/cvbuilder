## 10. Resume Tailoring Rules

- For German job descriptions, write German resume bullets in clear professional German; avoid overly complex phrasing.
- For English job descriptions, write concise English bullets with strong action verbs and grounded technical detail.
- Match the resume title and summary to the job description, but keep experience claims anchored in existing source evidence.
- Prefer “JV / alliance integration” wording unless the target file already uses a specific company name.
- Keep Ford bullets focused on the target role; do not dump all connected vehicle, compliance, ADAS, and cloud details into every resume.
- Use Continental and Maruti Suzuki as supporting experience unless the job is validation, systems engineering, ECU, or test-architecture heavy.
- Do not invent metrics. Use metrics already present in source files, such as over 1,000 fleet vehicles, around 100 signals, around 20 event types, 14 Fleet events, 99%+ data accuracy, and modular HiL cost/time savings when relevant.
- Use AI/GenAI/RAG only when the target role asks for it, and frame it as practical concepts, solution design, evaluation awareness, and knowledge retrieval rather than deep research ownership.
- For cloud roles, emphasize GCP and cloud-native transferable principles. Mention AWS/Azure carefully unless a newer source confirms direct hands-on depth.
- For SRE/observability roles, emphasize monitoring, alerting, dashboards, incident triage, production issue resolution, SLIs/SLOs/SLAs, and data/service reliability.
- For product roles, emphasize SAFe/ART delivery, backlog, requirements, acceptance criteria, stakeholder alignment, compliance, and product outcome clarity.
- For systems roles, emphasize ADAS/ECU, requirements, validation strategy, HiL/MiL/SiL, dSPACE, CAN, MATLAB/Simulink, and root-cause analysis.
- Preserve ATS readability and keep generated resume text selectable. Do not turn resume content into images.
